How to calculate speech time accurately

Divide the number of words in your script by your speaking pace. For example, 650 words at 130 words per minute takes about five minutes. Use this speaking time calculator or speech time estimator as a guide, then rehearse to account for pauses, transitions, and your natural rhythm.

Time in minutes = words ÷ WPM
02

Understanding speaking speeds

Many presentations land around 120–150 WPM. Slower delivery (100–120 WPM) can make complex ideas easier to follow; conversational storytelling may be quicker. Your audience and subject matter matter as much as the number.

Frequently asked questions

How many words are in a 5-minute speech?

At an average speaking pace of 130 WPM, a 5-minute speech is about 650 words. At 100 WPM, plan for around 500 words; at 160 WPM, around 800 words.

How long does it take to speak 1,000 words?

At 130 WPM, 1,000 words takes approximately 7 minutes and 42 seconds. A slower 100 WPM pace takes 10 minutes, while 160 WPM takes 6 minutes and 15 seconds.

How long is a 500-word speech?

A 500-word speech takes about 3 minutes and 51 seconds at the average 130 WPM pace. It takes 5 minutes at 100 WPM and about 3 minutes and 8 seconds at 160 WPM.

How many words are in a 10-minute speech?

At 130 words per minute, a 10-minute speech is about 1,300 words. Use the speech timer calculator above to set your own pace and check your script’s exact estimate.

How many words are in a 2-minute speech?

At 130 words per minute, a 2-minute speech is about 260 words. At a slower 100 WPM pace, plan for 200 words; at 160 WPM, plan for about 320 words.

How many words are in a 3-minute speech?

At 130 words per minute, a 3-minute speech is about 390 words. A slower 100 WPM delivery uses about 300 words, while a faster 160 WPM delivery uses about 480 words.

How many words are in a 15-minute speech?

At 130 words per minute, a 15-minute speech is about 1,950 words. Plan for about 1,500 words at 100 WPM or 2,400 words at 160 WPM.

How does a speech timer calculator estimate speaking time?

It divides your script’s word count by your selected speaking speed in words per minute. Choose a scenario from the dropdown or set a custom pace with the slider to convert words to minutes.

Can I use this as a speaking time calculator or words to minutes calculator?

Yes. Paste or type a script to see its word count and estimated speaking time. Choose a pace to convert the word count to minutes, or set a time limit to see your target word budget.

What is the difference between speech time and silent reading time?

Speech time is estimated from your speaking pace, commonly around 130 words per minute for a presentation. Silent reading usually moves faster, so this calculator uses an average of 230 words per minute for its reading-time estimate.

What is a good speaking pace for a presentation?

For many speakers, 120–150 words per minute is clear and comfortable. Try 130 WPM as a starting point, then adjust for your audience, topic, and natural delivery.

Does this calculator save or upload my text?

No. All calculations happen in your browser. Your draft is saved only in local storage on this device so you can return to it later.
