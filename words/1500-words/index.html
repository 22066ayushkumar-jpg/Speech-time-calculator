How Long Is a 1,500-Word Speech?

Speech Time Calculator · Free to use · 100% private · No sign-ups

The short answer

The arithmetic is simple and worth memorising: minutes = words ÷ WPM. A 1,500-word script therefore runs 11 minutes 32 seconds at the average 130 WPM presentation pace. The same 1,500 words stretch from a full 15 minutes at a measured 100 WPM toast pace to just 9 minutes 22 seconds at a fast 160 WPM pitch — a swing of nearly 6 minutes that is entirely about delivery, not content.

In manuscript terms that is exactly 3 single-spaced pages, or 6 double-spaced. If your slot has pauses, laughter or slide changes, plan for about 13 minutes 34 seconds of stage time instead — the 15% allowance is the difference between finishing calmly and finishing to a red timer.

What Fits in 1,500 Words?

A structure that fills 1,500 words without padding

One thousand five hundred words is one idea, properly developed: about 150 words of opening that states your claim, 1,200 words of body — three points of roughly 400 words each, or one story told well — and 150 words of close that lands the action you want. That is roughly 15–18 paragraphs and 75–100 sentences. If you are using slides, plan on about 12: one title slide and one per point, at just under a minute each.

Which slots is 1,500 words right for?

It is the perfect fit for a 12-minute slot at an average pace, a wedding toast or best-man speech of 10–15 minutes, a class presentation, or a podcast segment. It is not enough for a full 15-minute slot — that needs about 1,950 words — and it is far too much for a 5-minute slot, which holds only about 650 words. Match the script to the slot before you rehearse, not during.

Why the same 1,500 words never take the same time twice

Rehearsed reading is consistently faster than performed delivery. The gap is breathing, a slide change, a laugh you did not script, a sip of water. That is why the table above gives you a plan-for time with pauses built in: if your slot is exactly 12 minutes, a 1,500-word script at your natural 130 WPM leaves you about 28 seconds of breathing room — slow down, or trim to about 1,450 words and keep the margin comfortable.

1,500-word speech FAQs

How long is a 1,500-word speech?

About 11 minutes 32 seconds at 130 words per minute, the average presentation pace. At a slow 100 WPM it runs a full 15 minutes, and at a fast 160 WPM it is over in about 9 minutes 22 seconds. Add roughly 15% if you plan pauses, laughter or slide changes, which takes the 130 WPM delivery to about 13 minutes 34 seconds of stage time.

How many pages is a 1,500-word speech?

Exactly 3 single-spaced pages, or 6 double-spaced pages, in 12 pt Arial or Times New Roman with 1-inch margins. That assumes the standard manuscript convention of 500 words per single-spaced page and 250 words per double-spaced page.

How long does it take to read 1,500 words silently?

About 6 minutes 31 seconds at the average silent reading speed of 230 words per minute. Silent reading runs roughly 75% faster than speaking aloud, which is why a script that feels short on the page still fills nearly 12 minutes on stage.

Is 1,500 words enough for a 15-minute speech?

Not quite. A 15-minute slot at 130 WPM holds about 1,950 words, so a 1,500-word script leaves you roughly 3 minutes 28 seconds short. Either add about 450 words, or slow your pace toward 100 WPM — at a slow 100 WPM a 1,500-word script fills the full 15 minutes — and use the spare room for pauses and emphasis.
