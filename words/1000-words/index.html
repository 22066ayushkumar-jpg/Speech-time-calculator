How Long Is a 1,000-Word Speech?

Speech Time Calculator · Free to use · 100% private · No sign-ups

The short answer

The arithmetic is simple and worth memorising: minutes = words ÷ WPM. A 1,000-word script therefore runs 7 minutes 42 seconds at the average 130 WPM presentation pace. The same 1,000 words stretch from a full 10 minutes at a measured 100 WPM toast pace to just 6 minutes 15 seconds at a fast 160 WPM pitch — a swing of nearly 4 minutes that is entirely about delivery, not content.

In manuscript terms that is exactly 2 single-spaced pages, or 4 double-spaced. If your slot has pauses, laughter or slide changes, plan for about 9 minutes 3 seconds of stage time instead — the 15% allowance is the difference between finishing calmly and finishing to a red timer.

What Fits in 1,000 Words?

A structure that fills 1,000 words without padding

One thousand words is one idea, properly developed: about 100 words of opening that states your claim, 800 words of body — three points of roughly 265 words each, or one story told well — and 100 words of close that lands the action you want. That is roughly 10–12 paragraphs and 50–67 sentences. If you are using slides, plan on about 8: one title slide and one per point, at just under a minute each.

Which slots is 1,000 words right for?

It is the perfect fit for an 8-minute slot at an average pace, a wedding toast or best-man speech of 7–10 minutes, a class presentation, or a podcast segment. It is not enough for a full 10-minute slot — that needs about 1,300 words — and it is far too much for a 5-minute slot, which holds only about 650 words. Match the script to the slot before you rehearse, not during.

Why the same 1,000 words never take the same time twice

Rehearsed reading is consistently faster than performed delivery. The gap is breathing, a slide change, a laugh you did not script, a sip of water. That is why the table above gives you a plan-for time with pauses built in: if your slot is exactly 8 minutes, a 1,000-word script at your natural 130 WPM leaves you about 18 seconds of breathing room — slow down, or trim to about 950 words and keep the margin comfortable.

1,000-word speech FAQs

How long is a 1,000-word speech?

About 7 minutes 42 seconds at 130 words per minute, the average presentation pace. At a slow 100 WPM it runs a full 10 minutes, and at a fast 160 WPM it is over in about 6 minutes 15 seconds. Add roughly 15% if you plan pauses, laughter or slide changes, which takes the 130 WPM delivery to about 9 minutes 3 seconds of stage time.

How many pages is a 1,000-word speech?

Exactly 2 single-spaced pages, or 4 double-spaced pages, in 12 pt Arial or Times New Roman with 1-inch margins. That assumes the standard manuscript convention of 500 words per single-spaced page and 250 words per double-spaced page.

How long does it take to read 1,000 words silently?

About 4 minutes 21 seconds at the average silent reading speed of 230 words per minute. Silent reading runs roughly 75% faster than speaking aloud, which is why a script that feels short on the page still fills nearly 8 minutes on stage.

Is 1,000 words enough for a 10-minute speech?

Not quite. A 10-minute slot at 130 WPM holds about 1,300 words, so a 1,000-word script leaves you roughly 2 minutes 18 seconds short. Either add about 300 words, or slow your pace toward 100 WPM — at a slow 100 WPM a 1,000-word script fills the full 10 minutes — and use the spare room for pauses and emphasis.
