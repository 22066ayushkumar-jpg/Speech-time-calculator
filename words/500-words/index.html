How Long Is a 500-Word Speech?

Speech Time Calculator · Free to use · 100% private · No sign-ups

The short answer

The arithmetic is simple and worth memorising: minutes = words ÷ WPM. A 500-word script therefore runs 3 minutes 51 seconds at the average 130 WPM presentation pace. The same 500 words stretch from a full 5 minutes at a measured 100 WPM toast pace to just 3 minutes 8 seconds at a fast 160 WPM pitch — a swing of nearly 2 minutes that is entirely about delivery, not content.

In manuscript terms that is exactly 1 single-spaced page, or 2 double-spaced. If your slot has pauses, laughter or slide changes, plan for about 4 minutes 31 seconds of stage time instead — the 15% allowance is the difference between finishing calmly and finishing to a red timer.

What Fits in 500 Words?

A structure that fills 500 words without padding

Five hundred words is one idea, properly developed: about 50 words of opening that states your claim, 400 words of body — three points of roughly 130 words each, or one story told well — and 50 words of close that lands the action you want. That is roughly 5–6 paragraphs and 25–33 sentences. If you are using slides, plan on about 4: one title slide and one per point, at just under a minute each.

Which slots is 500 words right for?

It is the perfect fit for a 4-minute slot at an average pace, a wedding toast or best-man speech of 3–5 minutes, a class update, or a podcast segment. It is not enough for a full 5-minute slot — that needs about 650 words — and it is far too much for a 2-minute slot, which holds only about 260 words. Match the script to the slot before you rehearse, not during.

Why the same 500 words never take the same time twice

Rehearsed reading is consistently faster than performed delivery. The gap is breathing, a slide change, a laugh you did not script, a sip of water. That is why the table above gives you a plan-for time with pauses built in: if your slot is exactly 4 minutes, a 500-word script at your natural 130 WPM is already spoken for — slow down, or trim to about 440 words and keep the breathing room.

500-word speech FAQs

How long is a 500-word speech?

About 3 minutes 51 seconds at 130 words per minute, the average presentation pace. At a slow 100 WPM it runs a full 5 minutes, and at a fast 160 WPM it is over in about 3 minutes 8 seconds. Add roughly 15% if you plan pauses, laughter or slide changes, which takes the 130 WPM delivery to about 4 minutes 31 seconds of stage time.

How many pages is a 500-word speech?

Exactly 1 single-spaced page, or 2 double-spaced pages, in 12 pt Arial or Times New Roman with 1-inch margins. That assumes the standard manuscript convention of 500 words per single-spaced page and 250 words per double-spaced page.

How long does it take to read 500 words silently?

About 2 minutes 10 seconds at the average silent reading speed of 230 words per minute. Silent reading runs roughly 75% faster than speaking aloud, which is why a script that feels short on the page still fills nearly 4 minutes on stage.

Is 500 words enough for a 5-minute speech?

Not quite. A 5-minute slot at 130 WPM holds about 650 words, so a 500-word script leaves you roughly 1 minute 9 seconds short. Either add about 150 words, or slow your pace toward 100-110 WPM and use the spare room for pauses and emphasis.
