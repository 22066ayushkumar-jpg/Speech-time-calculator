How many words are in a 1 minute speech?

A 1-minute speech is typically about 130 words at an average speaking pace. If you speak slowly, aim for 100 words, and if you speak quickly, you can cover up to 160 words.

How many words are in a 3 minute speech?

A 3-minute speech usually contains around 390 words. For slower speakers, this is about 300 words, while faster speakers might reach 480 words.

How many words are in a 5 minute speech?

A 5-minute speech is approximately 650 words. Depending on your pacing and pauses, it can range from 500 to 800 words.

How many words are in a 10 minute speech?

A 10-minute speech contains around 1,300 words at an average conversational speed. Be sure to account for natural pauses for emphasis and audience reaction.

How many words are in a 15 minute speech?

A 15-minute speech typically requires around 1,950 words. Long speeches should include engaging transitions and varied pacing.

How many words are in a 20 minute speech?

A 20-minute speech is roughly 2,600 words. Remember that actual duration depends heavily on your speaking style, pauses, and the type of speech.

Speech Timing Across Languages

Speaking speed varies naturally between languages due to differences in syllable density and word length. For example, Spanish and Portuguese speakers often produce more syllables per second, but because those syllables pack less information individually, the overall rate of information delivery remains similar to English. This calculator supports English, Hindi, German, Spanish, and Portuguese. When estimating time for languages like Chinese, where a single character can represent an entire concept, words-per-minute (WPM) calculations change to characters-per-minute (CPM).
