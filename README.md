# 🎙️ Speech Time Calculator

A lightning-fast, 100% private, client-side web application designed to help speakers, presenters, podcasters, and students estimate speaking times, manage word budgets, and practice delivery with a built-in live timer and distraction-free teleprompter.

🌐 **Live Website:** [speechtimercalculator.com](https://speechtimercalculator.com/)

---

## ✨ Core Features

* **Instant Speaking & Reading Estimates:** Watch word counts, character counts, sentence counts, paragraphs, and estimated speech durations update in real-time as you type or paste your script.
* **Presets & Custom Paces:** Choose from popular speaking scenarios (TED Talk, Business Presentation, Academic Lecture, Wedding Toast, Podcast, Elevator Pitch, Commercial Voice-Over) or fine-tune your exact speed using the slider (60 to 400 WPM).
* **Target Word Budget:** Enter your exact time limit (3 to 20+ minutes) to instantly calculate your target word budget and keep your writing on track.
* **Interactive Practice Timer:** Rehearse your draft with built-in start, pause, reset, and lap tracking to compare your live performance against estimated pacing.
* **Distraction-Free Teleprompter:** Switch to focus mode with adjustable text sizing and hands-free auto-scrolling speed to practice your delivery smoothly.
* **Multilingual Counting Modes:** Supports word-based counting (WPM) for English, Spanish, German, Portuguese, and Hindi, alongside character-based counting (CPM) for Chinese, Japanese, and Korean scripts.
* **Export & Share:** Quickly copy a clean time summary or download your script with estimated timestamps inserted between paragraphs.
* **100% Private & Secure:** Built entirely client-side. Your scripts and drafts never leave your device and are saved securely via browser `localStorage` without requiring accounts, sign-ups, or server uploads.
