5-Minute Speech

Speech Time Calculator · Free to use · 100% private · No sign-ups

A 5-minute speech is about 650 words at a steady 130 words per minute — the pace most presenters use when they want an audience to follow without leaning forward. Speak slowly at 100 WPM and 500 words fills the slot; talk at a fast TED-style 160 WPM and you can use around 800 words. Write 550-650 words so applause, breathing room, and a slide change still fit inside five minutes. STAGE BUFFER

Write to 85% of the slot

Rehearsed reading is faster than performed delivery. Write to about 553 words — 85 percent of the 650-word budget above — so your 5 min 0 sec of text lands inside the slot with room for pauses, a slide change, breathing, and audience reaction. At 130 WPM the buffered script reads in 4 min 15 sec.

Questions about 5-minute speech

How many words is a 5-minute speech?

About 650 words at the average presentation pace of 130 words per minute. Slower, formal delivery at 100 WPM needs around 500 words, and an energetic TED-style 160 WPM carries roughly 800 words in the same five minutes.

Can I read 650 words in 5 minutes?

Yes, but you should plan for less. Applying the 85 percent stage buffer means writing about 550 words, which leaves 45 seconds inside your five minutes for pauses, breathing, slide changes, and audience reaction.

How many pages is a 5-minute speech?

A 650-word script is about 1.3 pages single-spaced or 2.6 pages double-spaced in standard manuscript formatting. Most speakers read one double-spaced page in just under two minutes at a normal pace.
